## What the baseline showed

Most retrospective recommendations describe behavior that agents already chose in a single-decision scenario. The retrospective also suspected that inconsistent application, not missing text, caused those problems. A single-turn scenario cannot reproduce drift over hours of waiting, so these passes do not show that the long-run behavior is fixed.

The baseline found one real gap: review-changes did not ask for a per-caller comparison of old and new behavior, or for a report of what the review traced. The revision adds both.

The batch-corrections agent found an error in `skills/shared/review.md`. It said that a push does not trigger a new review automatically, but repository automation can do so. The revision corrects it.

The revision does not add the recommendations that already passed. Contract-test precision stays in the subject project's guidance, as the retrospective recommends.
